# SEHAI Mapping — Standard 8: Understandability and Accessibility vs. Standard 1 (MNI)

**Score:** Supports 2 · Partially supports 6 · Silent 22 · Contradicts 0 (of 30)

## Findings

**1. The most independent standard yet — 22 silences.** S7 governed output truthfulness; S8 governs output *form*, one step further from MNI's input-side territory. The standard draws its own boundary: "Accuracy and Honesty ensures that what the system says is calibrated to its actual certainty. Understandability and Accessibility ensures that what the system says can actually be understood."

**2. Its MNI connections run entirely through comprehension-as-precondition:** MNI-15's disclosures, MNI-26's benchmark, and MNI-30's effectiveness measure only function if communication lands. S8 supplies the landing requirement. Its single most quotable line doubles as a thesis-ready aphorism: "Information that cannot be understood has not actually been given."

**3. Two structural doctrines worth flagging:** (a) *asymmetric adaptivity* — the system may adjust complexity UPWARD for sophisticated users but must never require users to request simplification (default = accessible); (b) *stress-aware communication* — reduced processing capacity under stress is treated as a design constraint, favoring short, direct opening-phase communication. Both are testable.

**4. First explicit equity foundation in the suite** — equal standard of engagement across literacy, language, disability, and stress conditions, with a named disparate-impact mechanism.

**5. Zero contradictions.** The simplicity-vs-completeness tension is resolved internally via progressive introduction, consistent with MNI-09.

## New obligations S8 adds (synthesis ledger)

- Plain language by default; jargon only on indicated preference/familiarity.
- Asymmetric adaptivity: complexity adjusts upward only; accessible register is the unprompted default.
- Language accessibility: accurate translation / multilingual parity.
- Disability & cognitive accommodation: screen-reader compatibility, no visual-only cues, tolerance for slow/non-linear interaction.
- Stress-aware communication: short and direct especially at interaction start.
- Understandability Benchmark (TI-11): "understood by someone with no medical, legal, or technical background, reading it once, under stress."

## Candidate revisions for MNI v1.1

- **MNI-15** pass criterion could add a comprehension check (disclosure ≠ comprehension).
- **TI-1** (opening questions) could adopt the TI-11 stress test.
- **MNI-09** could cite progressive *explanation* alongside progressive disclosure.
