# SEHAI Mapping — Standard 2: Purpose Transparency vs. Standard 1 (MNI)

**Verdict legend:** Supports · Partially supports · Silent · Contradicts

**Score:** Supports 10 · Partially supports 10 · Silent 10 · Contradicts 0 (of 30)

## Findings

**1. Zero contradictions.** Purpose Transparency is fully complementary to MNI — by design, since the standard declares itself MNI's 'direct operational partner.'

**2. Strongest overlap** with the justification/communication cluster (MNI-03, 06, 11, 12, 19) and the evaluation cluster (MNI-25, 26): PT converts MNI's internal necessity test into a per-request communicative act, and supplies the mechanism that makes benchmark question 4 answerable.

**3. Total silence** on MNI's scope-and-sequence core (MNI-02, 07, 08, 09, 14, 17, 20, 24): PT says nothing about how much to collect, when to ask, whether enough questions are asked, or data granularity. These remain MNI-only obligations — PT alone cannot make a system MNI-compliant.

**4. New obligations PT adds beyond MNI** (to carry into Standards 3–13 mapping and the final synthesis): plain-language requirement; same-time pairing (no hyperlink/buried policy); specificity test (boilerplate 'to help you' = failure); stated-use vs. actual-use consistency; right to ask 'why' at any point; comprehension benchmark ('could the individual repeat the reason?').

## Candidate revisions for MNI v1.1 (flagged, not yet applied)

- **MNI-15** could adopt PT's specificity/plain-language tests so disclosures can't be boilerplate.
- **MNI-22** could add PT's 'stated purpose must match actual downstream use' as an explicit pass criterion.
- **TI-3** could add PT's comprehension benchmark as the operational test for MNI-11.
